Market context

Why hospitality data fragments

The pattern is common across the industry: guest data is spread across systems bought at different times for different jobs.

Systems of record grew up apart

The PMS owns stays and rooms, the CRM owns loyalty and preferences, POS owns spend, and Operations owns who looks after whom. None of them was designed to agree with the others.

Identity is the weak link

Free-text membership numbers, local phone formats and mistyped emails mean the same guest looks like different people. Charges on open tabs arrive with only a name.

AI raises the stakes

An assistant that reads raw systems repeats their contradictions confidently. Governed data products give it one answer, with masking and approval rules applied before it speaks.

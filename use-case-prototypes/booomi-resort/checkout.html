Act 3 · Check-out, settled and turned around by Boomi

One tap to leave. The bill fixed, paid, and the room ready again.

The guest checks out on the app. Boomi builds the final bill with the same subprocess the folio data product uses, fixes the data-quality problems before the guest pays, captures the card, and tells eight systems the guest has left. Then housekeeping events turn the room around until it is ready for the next guest.

What is real here

    
When the demo server is running with Boomi credentials, Check out & pay calls the deployed process
      CR_P6_Guest_CheckOut_Orchestration through /ws/simple/createGuestcheckout,
      and each housekeeping button posts an event to CR_P7_Housekeeping_Room_Status through
      /ws/simple/updateRoomstatus. The final bill is built by a Process Call to
      [SUB] CR Folio Source Extract, the same subprocess behind the consolidated-folio data product.
      The Data Hub steps are live: the golden record is resolved by loyalty id, and on check-out its current room is cleared with
      source OPERA_PMS. The payment gateway, Opera, door lock, Salesforce, Simphony, Finance GL,
      housekeeping and guest messaging are stub sends: Message steps that build the exact payload, where a real connector
      would be swapped in. Offline, or opened as a file, the page replays recorded traces of the same processes.

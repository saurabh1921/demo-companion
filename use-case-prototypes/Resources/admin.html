Before you present

  1. Sign in (top right), then open the sign-in menu and choose Reset demo. It puts the fleet back to its start-of-shift status in Data Hub.
  2. Acts 1 and 3 write machine status to Data Hub. Use Reset demo again between rehearsals to set HT-214 back to available.
  3. Keep the operator app and supervisor desk open in their own tabs, and switch to them after Act 1 and Act 3.
  4. The persona and operator you pick on one page carry over to the others in the same browser.

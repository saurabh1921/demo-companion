Act 1 · Shift start, orchestrated by Boomi

One badge tap at the crib. The right truck, safely, in six systems.

06:00 at Red Ridge. An operator badges in on the cab tablet. Boomi checks the site induction, the haul licence and the fitness-for-work gate, reads the governed haul fleet live from Data Hub, scores every truck on licence, pre-start, service headroom and pit, then tells dispatch, Data Hub, access control, SAP time recording and the tablet. A failed pre-start tags the truck out with a ServiceNow incident; a failed check stands the operator down with a ServiceNow case that records the outcome only.

What is real here

    
With the demo server running, Badge in calls the deployed process BR_P5_Shift_Start_Orchestration
      through /ws/simple/createBrshiftstart. The Data Hub steps are live: the haul fleet is read from the
      equipmentmaster golden records, and the assigned truck is written back as OPERATING
      (a tagged-out truck as TAGGED_OUT) in a FLEET_MGMT batch. The crew roster check,
      dispatch, access control, SAP time recording, ServiceNow and the tablet are stub sends: Message steps that build the exact payload,
      where a real connector is swapped in. No health detail is ever sent or stored: the ServiceNow case carries the outcome only. Offline, or
      opened as a file, the page replays traces recorded from the live process, and the badge says so.

Reading this as a Source-to-Pay practice

The flow above is the demo. These are the four things that tend to matter once it stops being a demo.

Reuse across clients

Both flows are 15 components in total. Swapping Coupa for Ariba, or Fusion for SAP, changes the connection and the map — the routing, error handling and writeback survive intact.

One build, many tenants

The endpoints here are sandbox URLs. Moving to a live tenant is a base-URL and credential change through environment extensions, not a rebuild, so one deployment serves dev, test and each client tenant.

Where Peppol slots in

The supplier flow already carries the ABN across as tax-id — the identifier a participant ID is built from. An Access Point send or receive sits beside the Fusion connector on the same canvas.

Where the cost per invoice sits

The branch is the interesting shape. PO-backed invoices three-way match and clear themselves; the non-PO path is where the manual handling lives, and where matching and approval automation earns its keep.

What is real here. The shape names, their order, the per-shape millisecond timings, the source payloads, the transformed output and the log lines are all taken from verified executions of two deployed Boomi processes (S2P - Coupa Invoice to Oracle Fusion AP and S2P - Oracle Fusion Supplier to Coupa). The transformation you see applied in the browser is the same field mapping the deployed maps perform.

What is a prototype. The left and right panes are illustrative stand-ins for the Coupa and Oracle Fusion user interfaces, built for this walkthrough — they are not those products. Suppliers, invoices, ABNs and account codes are fabricated demo data, localised to AUD and Australian addresses for this session. The deployed processes call a sandbox echo endpoint in place of live tenants; pointing them at real tenants is a base-URL change on the two connections.

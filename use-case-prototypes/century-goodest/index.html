Every Goodest packet asks how the dog liked it.

The owner scans the QR on the packet and answers five quick taps in English or Tagalog. The batch code rides in the link, so R&D can trace feedback straight back to a production run.

Link in the QR:

Submit the survey on the phone and the record saved to Boomi DataHub shows up here.

What happens behind the scan

  1. Scan. The QR opens the survey with ?batch= already set. The owner is never asked for it.
  2. Answer. Consent, five tap questions and an optional comment. No names, numbers, emails or device IDs.
  3. Boomi. An Integration process validates the answers, removes phone numbers and emails from the comment, and writes the Response and Answer golden records to Boomi DataHub.
  4. R&D. The Goodest R&D Insights agent and dashboard read the same DataHub model. They report aggregates only and hide any cell with fewer than 5 responses.

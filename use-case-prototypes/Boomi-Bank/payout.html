Act 3 · Loan payout and closure, orchestrated by Boomi

One tap to pay off. The data fixed first, the money moved, the security released.

The customer pays out their loans in the app. Boomi resolves them in Data Hub, checks compliance, builds the final payout with the same subprocess the positions data product uses, fixes the data-quality problems before any money moves, transfers the funds and tells nine systems the loans are closed. Then registry events release the security, step by step.

What is real here

    
The payout review in the app comes from the live positions data product
      BB_P3_Consolidated_Positions_API (/ws/simple/queryBbpositions), and every
      security-release button posts a live event to BB_P7_Security_Release_Status
      (/ws/simple/updateBbsecurityrelease). Pay out my loans calls
      BB_P6_Loan_Payout_Closure_Orchestration through /ws/simple/createBbloanpayout;
      its Data Hub steps (resolve the customer, clear the active loans) are live once the Boomi-Bank repository credentials work, and until
      then the page replays a trace of the same contract, labelled REPLAYED. The final payout is built by a Process Call to
      [SUB] BB Account & Loan Source Extract, the same subprocess behind the positions product. Payments,
      core banking, PEXA / PPSR, direct debits, the credit bureau, Salesforce, the ledger, documents and messaging are stub sends:
      Message steps that build the exact payload, where a real connector would be swapped in.

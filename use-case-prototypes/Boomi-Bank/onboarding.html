One application in the app. Seven systems set up.

Mia Nguyen applies on her phone. What is real here

    
When the demo server is running with Boomi credentials, Open my accounts calls the deployed process
      BB_P5_Digital_Onboarding_Orchestration through /ws/simple/createBbonboarding.
      The Data Hub steps are live: Mia is looked up by email in the Boomi-Bank / customermaster repository, and her
      golden record is created with two batches, sources CORE_BANKING and AML_KYC.
      Her driver licence number is hashed by the process and never returned. Document verification, screening, core banking, the card
      platform, NPP, Salesforce and messaging are stub sends: Message steps that build the exact payload, the point where a
      real connector is swapped in. Until the Data Hub repository credentials are in place, or offline, or opened as a file, the page
      replays recorded traces of the same process (computed from the contract until live ones are recorded), and the badge says why.

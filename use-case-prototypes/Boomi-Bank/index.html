Market context

Why banking customer data fragments

Banks buy systems for different jobs at different times, and each one keeps its own customer: the ledger, the CRM, the cards, compliance.

Systems of record grew up apart

Core banking owns the legal name, address and loans; Salesforce owns segment, contact and preferences; AML/KYC owns the review date and risk rating; the roster owns who the banker is.

Identity is the weak link

Free-text customer numbers, local phone formats, mistyped emails and differently formatted licence numbers make one customer look like several. A business card or a BPAY payment arrives with only a name.

AI raises the stakes

An assistant reading raw systems repeats their contradictions. Governed data products give it one answer, with ID numbers withheld, waivers routed for approval and personal advice off limits.

Guardrails

  • Never reveals full account or card numbers, TFNs or ID document numbers (tool output is screened too).
  • Fee or break-cost waivers over AUD 200 need supervisor approval.
  • Denied topic: personal financial advice. General information only (ASIC general vs personal advice).

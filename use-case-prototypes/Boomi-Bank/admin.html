Before you present

  1. Sign in (top right), then open the sign-in menu and choose Reset demo. It restores the demo customers' loans in Data Hub and re-runs Mia's onboarding.
  2. Act 3 closes the customer's loans in Data Hub. Use Reset demo again between rehearsals to restore the customers' loans.
  3. Keep the customer app and RM desk open in their own tabs, and switch to them after Act 1 and Act 2.
  4. The customer or caller you pick on one page carry over to the others in the same browser.

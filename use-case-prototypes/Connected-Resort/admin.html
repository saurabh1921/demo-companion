Before you present

  1. Sign in (top right), then open the sign-in menu and choose Reset demo. It checks the demo guests back in to restore their rooms in Data Hub.
  2. Check-in and check-out write to Data Hub. Use Reset demo again between rehearsals.
  3. Keep the guest app and concierge desk open in their own tabs, and switch to them after Act 1 and during Act 2.
  4. Set the guest toggle once. The guest app and concierge desk open on the same guest.
